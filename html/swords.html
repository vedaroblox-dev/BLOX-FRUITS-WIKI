Swords & Fighting Styles

Aside from fruits, players rely on melee fighting styles (which also increase energy/stamina) and legendary swords.

Top Fighting Styles

  • Godhuman: The ultimate V2 melee style combining all previous masteries, famous for fast PvP combo starters.
  • Sanguine Art: Unlocked in the Third Sea via Leviathan hunting; features life-steal abilities.
  • Sharkman Karate: Fast M1 attack speed, making it a favorite for Buddha fruit grinders.
  • Electric Claw: High mobility and stun potential.

Legendary & Mythical Swords

  • Cursed Dual Katana (CDK): A Mythical sword forged by combining Yama and Tushita; deals massive burst damage.
  • True Triple Katana (TTK): Obtained by mastering Shisui, Wando, and Saddi in the Second Sea.
  • Dark Blade (Yoru): A Gamepass Mythical sword with wide slashes and high upgrade potential.
  • Shark Anchor: Crafted from Terror Shark drops; excels at pulling enemies into combos.
