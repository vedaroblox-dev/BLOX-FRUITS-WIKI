Blox Fruits Categories & Notable Fruits

Blox Fruits grant supernatural abilities at the cost of losing the ability to swim in water. They are divided into three main types: Natural, Elemental (Logia), and Beast (Zoan).

Fruit Name Type Rarity Best Known For
Kitsune Beast Mythical High speed, blue fire tails, and top-tier PvP/grinding utility.
Leopard Beast Mythical Fast transformations, high mobility, and aggressive melee combos.
Dough Elemental Mythical Unmatched stun combos and crowd control once fully awakened.
Buddha Beast Legendary Massive hitbox and damage reduction; the undisputed best fruit for grinding levels.
Light Elemental Rare Fastest flight speed and elemental immunity; ideal for First Sea players.
