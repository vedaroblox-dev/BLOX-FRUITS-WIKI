What is Blox Fruits?

Inspired by the popular anime and manga series One Piece, Blox Fruits is an action-adventure RPG on Roblox. Players train to become master swordsmen or powerful Blox Fruit users as they sail across vast oceans, complete quests, and defeat challenging bosses.

Core Gameplay Mechanics

  • Leveling & Stats: Earn EXP from quests to upgrade Melee, Defense, Sword, Gun, and Blox Fruit stats.
  • Trading: Visit the Café (Second Sea) or Mansion (Third Sea) to trade physical fruits with other players.
  • Awakenings: Complete special Raids to unlock stronger, awakened versions of specific fruits.
