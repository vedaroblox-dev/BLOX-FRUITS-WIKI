Progression: The Three Seas

Your journey in Blox Fruits spans three distinct oceans, each unlocking new islands, bosses, and endgame features as you level up.

1. First Sea (Levels 1 – 700)

Where every player begins. Focuses on learning basic combat, buying your first Aura (Haki) and Instinct (Observation), and defeating the Ice Admiral at Level 700 to unlock the passage to the next sea.

2. Second Sea (Levels 700 – 1,500)

Unlocks major features including Fruit Trading at the Café, Fruit Awakenings (Raids), Sea Beasts, Race V2/V3 upgrades, and Factory Raids. Defeating Don Swan and rip_indra grants access to the Third Sea.

3. Third Sea (Levels 1,500 – Max(Current max level:3000))

The endgame ocean featuring Sea Events (Leviathan, Kitsune Shrine, Terror Sharks), Race V4 awakenings at the Temple of Time, weapon enchanting, and elite PvP bounty hunting.
